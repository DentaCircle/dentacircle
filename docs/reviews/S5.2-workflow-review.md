# S5.2 workflow review

Reviewed 2026-11-03 against PRODUCT.md, the S5.1/S5.2 briefs, messaging design and current patient/appointment/follow-up/recall services. Six further failure-scenario corrections were authorised by the developer on 2026-11-04 and incorporated below. This remains the design record for the implemented S5.2a/S5.2b workflow; runtime verification is recorded in the slice briefs and PR checks.

## Findings and disposition

| Gap | Required behavior | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A registered patient may already have a visit; retry protection alone does not prevent booking another non-overlapping visit accidentally. | Show upcoming visits and require an explicit additional-booking choice, including a new staff booking during the dialogue. | Developer selected show/allow explicit additional booking; added B2/B6. |
| A patient may not know the right clinician/date before seeing availability. | After appointment-type selection, offer earliest available times, with filters available afterward. | Developer selected earliest first; added B3, with a bounded seven-day default. |
| Patient needs help or staff replies while the bot is still driving menus. | Pause automation, show Needs reception/Staff handling, and require explicit resume. Invitation is a distinct purpose. | Developer selected pause/flag; added B5 and state/control contracts. |
| STOP currently ends a session but leaves room for the next message/expiry to restart it. | Persist stopped mode outside the expiring session; BOOK/staff resume re-enables it. | Clarified B4/B5 and recovery tests. |
| A delayed queued slot message can arrive after expiry, a newer menu, handoff or booking confirmation. | Order sends per contact and suppress obsolete queued menus using session/version/mode/expiry. | Added A4/B7 and listed the needed metadata/states. |
| Repeating an old confirmation can report the original booking after reception moves/cancels it. | Read the current appointment for STATUS/repeated code; do not rebook. | Clarified B7. |
| A DB overlap error can abort the transaction and lose the incoming message instead of returning a recovery choice. | Keep inbound/recovery in the outer transaction and roll back attempted booking writes only. | Clarified B6/B7 and conflict tests. |
| Reception may register/link a shared or changed phone number that the first contact model cannot represent. | Keep registration saved, show link conflict, and use normal reception booking. Do not auto-reassign identity. | Explicit v1 limitation in B8 and booking contract; dependent/relinking support remains out of scope. |
| A patient may not know which appointment type to choose, or may ask a clinical question. | Provide Not sure/STAFF help, without inferring a procedure or answering clinically. | Clarified B3/B5; clinician answers remain outside automation. |
| An invitation may fail, leaving reception uncertain whether the patient was registered or whether to retry. | Persist registration independently, use the explicit linked number, show saved/send states and preserve request identity. | Existing requirement retained and tests expanded in B8. |
| Registration itself may commit but lose its response; POST /patients currently has no idempotency key. | Show uncertain-save feedback and direct staff to check the patient list before retrying. | Added B8 feedback. Exactly-once registration remains a separate change; this review does not claim to provide it. |
| Delayed BOOK arrives after a later STOP or staff takeover; event deduplication alone does not establish ordering. | Preserve the pause unless fresh intent is established. Missing/coarse/tied timestamps use a new BOOK code bound to the current automation version; ambiguous STOP/STAFF conservatively pauses. | Added B4/B5, version/challenge metadata and reversed-delivery/expiry/replay tests. The OpenWA default uses the fresh-code fallback. |
| The initial booking result waits in the queue while staff moves/cancels the visit. | Refresh any time/status claim immediately before dispatch, preserving the reference and as-of time; freeze the body after claim. Never recreate the visit. | Added B7 and A's pre-claim rendering step; test initial confirmation and STATUS/repeated codes after staff mutation before dispatch. |
| A crash-left sending attempt can strand all later replies behind the one-in-flight rule. | Persist claim ownership/deadline and use an exclusive contact fence. Prove the owner inactive, mark unknown without resending, then advance; a deadline alone cannot overlap live owners. | Added A6, claim metadata and killed-owner/next-reply/live-owner/stale-finalization tests; B7 requires working STATUS after recovery. |
| Acknowledged visit ids remain unchanged when staff reschedules or changes their status. | Compare the complete membership and relevant fields; changed visits invalidate acknowledgement and confirmation. Staff appointment writes share patient-first locks. | Added A3 and B2/B6 snapshot/locking contracts; test same-id reschedule and races with move/cancel/status. No new appointment version column or staff permission is added. |
| Unrecognised free text during booking can stay unnoticed while automation remains active. | Deterministically flag needs_staff, invalidate choices and send one fixed handoff reply without classifying or answering clinical text. Recognisable invalid codes retain safe recovery. | Added B5 and free-text/code/paused/post-booking tests; no NLP, notification service or immediate-response promise. |
| Visit/type/clinician lists can exceed five entries or 2,000 characters; the current upcoming-visits read caps at 20. | Page every list completely with bounded reads/messages, safe labels and stable token-bound cursors. Never treat the staff read's cap as the complete acknowledgement set. | Added B2/B3 and dialogue-only complete reads; test long labels, more than five entries, more than 20 visits and no unseen acknowledgement/omissions. Existing staff endpoint behavior stays unchanged. |

## Scope and approval

The developer accepted the first three product choices during the original review and requested incorporation of all six follow-up findings on 2026-11-04. S5.2a/S5.2b remain approved, including the listed claim/version/challenge/snapshot and locking refinements; no dependency, external service, new patient registration or clinical automation is added. All schema/API amendments are enumerated in the child briefs. If the combined B changes exceed one reviewable change, the builder must propose a split before code; this review does not authorize expanding it further.

Full behavior and examples: [booking contract](../whatsapp-booking.md). Build order was S5.1 → S5.2a → S5.2b. S5.1 is sealed and S5.2a/S5.2b are implemented; deferred first-contact invitations are tracked separately in S5.2c.

## Verification limits

Checked the draft against current code: patient creation already returns an id; availability is currently clinician/day based; upcoming visits and patient busy spans already exist; follow-up/recall services atomically book their source; audit requires a staff actor today. The upcoming read caps at 20, and appointment reschedule/cancel/status currently take appointment locks before any patient lock, so the explicit complete-read and patient-first refinements remain necessary. The planned actor migration remains necessary. Changes in this review are documentation only; use the child briefs' tests and live proof before claiming the workflow works. The [booking contract](../whatsapp-booking.md#verification-scenarios) maps all six follow-up findings to required automated scenarios.
