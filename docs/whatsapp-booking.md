# WhatsApp booking contract

Detail for approved [S5.2 scope](../.shipit/slices/S5.2-coordinator-booking.md), [foundation](../.shipit/slices/S5.2a-whatsapp-action-foundation.md) and [conversation](../.shipit/slices/S5.2b-whatsapp-self-booking.md). Review corrections authorised 2026-11-04. These are build requirements, not implemented behavior. S5.1 is still a prerequisite.

## Entry and identity

The patient must already be registered and active, with an explicitly linked, allowlisted test WhatsApp number on the configured clinic account. Staff can send the invitation immediately after registration/linking or later from the conversation. The message identifies the clinic and says “Reply BOOK to choose an appointment time.” The patient can also start by messaging the clinic directly.

The current patient flow uses numbered replies to the specific WhatsApp message. After selecting an appointment type, the patient chooses the earliest available date or one of the next seven clinic-local dates, then chooses any available clinician or a specific clinician before seeing times. This supersedes the older times-first and visible-code examples below; the current-menu, pause and final-booking safeguards still apply.

Registration, number linking and external sending are separate steps. A link/send failure must not look like failed registration or cause a second registration. If the registration response itself is uncertain, direct staff to check the patient list before retrying. The current POST /patients has no idempotency key; this slice improves that feedback but cannot guarantee exactly-once registration. A registration-idempotency guarantee needs a separate approved change.

Never silently copy patient.phone into a contact or change the contact when that field is edited. S5.1 supports one patient per number and immutable links: shared family numbers, number corrections and changed numbers need reception's ordinary booking workflow. Relinking/dependent identity needs a later approved slice. Unlinked/unknown/inactive senders do not enter self-booking or receive automated patient details.

## Patient path

1. A first greeting gives a short welcome with BOOK and STAFF instructions. BOOK enters booking. HELP lists supported commands. Finishing a visit booking or saying thanks never starts another one automatically.
2. Read this patient's complete current upcoming visits through bounded pages. Show reference, local date/time, type and clinician. If any exist, offer the explicit Book another visit choice only after the complete list has been presented. Do not treat a repeated BOOK or confirmation code as that acknowledgement. Capture ids, starts/ends, clinician/type ids and status, rather than ids alone. Before final booking, compare the complete current snapshot under the patient lock: new/removed visits or changed fields invalidate acknowledgement and the old confirmation. Show refreshed details, including a moved/cancelled previously acknowledged visit, and require a fresh additional-booking choice, summary and confirmation when visits remain. Never use the staff endpoint's 20-visit limit as a complete set.
3. Let the patient choose a general visit or a scheduled/contacted follow-up or recall. A booked source links to its current visit. Show source labels/due dates rather than clinical care details. A general visit does not satisfy a queue row by inference.
4. Let the patient choose an active appointment type. “Not sure” requests staff help; do not diagnose, recommend a procedure or infer duration/type from clinical text. The appointment type supplies duration through the existing rules.
5. Default to the earliest available choices across active clinicians over today through six days ahead, in clinic-local time. Search order is start time then clinician id. Exclude the patient's overlapping blocking visits. Each choice identifies clinician, date, start/end and timezone. Keep at most five choices within the 2,000-character limit; MORE advances, and Change date/clinician/type starts fresh choices. An explicit date still respects the existing 365-day horizon. No matching slot provides another-date/reception options rather than a dead end.
6. Selection sends a summary and fresh CONFIRM code. Nothing is reserved. Confirmation revalidates rules and creates the scheduled visit through the existing service; any selected follow-up/recall is booked in that transaction. The automated confirmation is sent only after commit.

Menus and summaries carry current short tokens; bare numbers without the required token are re-prompted safely. Stateful paging/choice commands also carry the current token, so delayed MORE/filter replies cannot skip forward in a different menu. Bind tokens to clinic/account/contact/session, menu version and contact automation version. Each valid conversation step sets a 15-minute expiry. MORE/filter changes rotate the version, invalidating old choices. A stale code never acquires a new meaning. BOOK resumes an active flow without silently selecting Book another visit; START OVER clears active choices.

Pagination applies to existing visits, sources, appointment types, clinician filters and slots. Each entire message, including instructions/tokens/navigation, fits S5.1's 2,000-character validator and contains at most five entries. Reduce the page size or shorten display labels safely when needed; codes remain unambiguous and the selected summary shows the full details. One inbound paging command produces at most one automatic reply. Use stable version-bound cursors; changes that invalidate a list restart it rather than skip unseen entries. Do not silently truncate a list or mark unseen visits acknowledged. Keep database reads/pages bounded while walking the complete set; the existing staff endpoint and its cap remain unchanged.

## Reception and stop behavior

Contact automation state is persistent and independent of the expiring booking session:

| Trigger | State | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STAFF / Talk to reception | needs_staff | End active choices, acknowledge once and show Needs reception in inbox/history. |
| Staff Take over or ordinary staff reply | staff_handling | End active choices; free text stays for staff without scheduling menus. |
| STOP | stopped | End active choices, acknowledge without cancelling any appointment; no timeout-based restart. |
| Patient BOOK while active | active | Start/resume the flow; never count it as acknowledgement of another visit. |
| Patient BOOK while paused | unchanged | Use the freshness check below; if ordering is uncertain, issue a BOOK code without clearing the pause. |
| Patient BOOK with current resume code | active | Clear pause/flag and offer a fresh flow, including existing visits. |
| Staff Resume | active | Clear pause/flag and old choices; next patient interaction can start fresh. Sending an invitation remains explicit. |

Pausing never discards chat history. An old CONFIRM while paused cannot book. STATUS is a narrow read-only command available without restarting the booking flow. Staff invitations are marked as booking_invitation, so they do not accidentally take over; an invitation does not clear an existing pause. Changing control state is audited with the real initiating actor and field names only, without adding patient timeline kinds. Replaying a saved staff-send request returns the same attempt and must not take over again after a patient resumes.

### Delayed commands and fresh resume

Deduplication does not establish event order. Persist a monotonic contact automation_version and automation_changed_at; every STOP, handoff, takeover or resume control transition invalidates older choices/challenges. Record distinct incoming events even when their command is rejected as stale. Never infer freshness from callback arrival order or from absent, tied, coarse or incomparable provider timestamps. Provider-sent time remains provenance; it is not by itself a reliable cross-channel order against staff controls.

The default for this OpenWA demo is conservative: a bare BOOK received while stopped/needs_staff/staff_handling leaves that mode unchanged and issues a fresh `BOOK <code>` challenge. Only a reply with that current code, bound to contact/account/session/automation version and expiring after 15 minutes, can clear the pause. A still-valid pending challenge can be reused for repeated bare BOOK without extending its original expiry. This is the fallback whenever reliable ordering cannot be established; do not assume the prerequisite bridge supplies such ordering. A known-stale BOOK cannot enable automation. A delayed STOP/STAFF with ambiguous order may conservatively pause again; reject it without a state change if reliable ordering proves it obsolete.

No patient/visit details or slot choices appear in the resume challenge. Dispatch it only if the contact is still eligible, the same paused automation version remains current and the challenge is unexpired; otherwise mark it superseded. Accepting the code or staff resume starts fresh choices and invalidates every previous challenge. Expired/replayed/wrong-contact codes cannot resume or book. Staff resume remains an explicit authorised action and does not send an invitation automatically.

Reception controls use the existing three messaging roles. Inbox/contact reads expose automation_state; no unread counters, push alerts, background notifications or guaranteed response time are added. Handoff copy must not claim staff have already been notified.

During an active booking flow, text outside the fixed command/menu grammar triggers needs_staff, invalidates choices and produces one generic handoff acknowledgement. Preserve the text for staff without classifying it as clinical or answering it. Copy says reception can review the message and clinical questions need a clinician; it promises neither notification nor an immediate response. Recognisable invalid/stale codes get safe recovery instructions instead of a handoff. Use a small explicit greeting/courtesy list (HI/HELLO/HEY/THANKS/THANK YOU, case-insensitive after trimming) for welcome/no-op behavior, with no NLP. While stopped/paused, ordinary free text stays in history without further automatic handoff replies. Casual conversation after a completed booking does not open a new booking flow.

## Booking and delivery recovery

- Duplicate provider events are acknowledged without processing the command again. Distinct repeated confirmations resolve the saved session/visit, with no new appointment, queue mutation or booking audit/timeline event.
- Every queued booking-result reply, including the first confirmation, STATUS and repeated codes, resolves the saved visit again immediately before dispatch. Staff reschedules show the new time; staff cancellation/no-show/completion shows current status. Preserve the reference even when a result is no longer scheduled; never send a cached “booked” claim or silently replace the visit. Persist the refreshed text with its as-of time before moving queued to sending, then freeze the body. No appointment/source write or second booking audit/timeline event occurs. If current details cannot be read, keep it queued for a bounded later check rather than send cached details. This describes truth at dispatch, not guaranteed delivery-time freshness.
- Source cancellation, patient deactivation, removed clinicians/types, changed duration/hours, conflicts or expired choices fail before business commit. Explain the scheduling problem with safe fixed copy and offer fresh choices or staff help. Reuse service rules.
- A recoverable conflict rolls back only the attempted booking, retaining the inbound message, failed choice transition and recovery reply in the outer transaction. A database commit failure returns S5.1's retryable callback failure; it does not acknowledge lost history.
- Per-contact sends follow persisted dispatch order. Before dispatch, recheck connection/account/allowlist/patient eligibility. Booking menus also require active automation, matching session/menu/automation versions and an unexpired menu. Obsolete queued menus become superseded without external sending. A resume challenge has its own paused-version/expiry guard. A committed booking result or a stop/handoff acknowledgement is not discarded by the menu guard; a result still requires the current-record refresh above.
- Staging a message and claiming it are distinct durable steps. Persist claim owner and send deadline; fence each contact to one live dispatcher across the bounded provider call, using an independent DB connection without business row locks or an open business transaction. A PostgreSQL session-level advisory lock can provide the fence. A crash before claim permits queued recovery; after claim never redispatch that attempt. Once a new dispatcher acquires the fence and proves the old owner inactive, mark the abandoned sending row unknown, record that state with its original initiator, and continue the next queued reply, including STATUS. Deadline expiry alone cannot override a live owner; stale-owner finalization must fail. Report a stuck live owner in a bounded step and continue processing other contacts. Operator recovery terminates the stuck owner before releasing its fence. Acceptance is not delivery/read confirmation.
- A lost outbound booking confirmation never undoes the appointment. The patient can use STATUS/repeat the code; reception can verify in calendar/history. Queued sends survive API restart and uncertainty remains visible. Do not hold patient/business transaction locks while calling the provider.

Use consistent lock order: patient, then contact when needed, then session/appointment/source. Staff creation/reschedule/cancel/status writes and queue booking must participate in the patient lock before appointment/source locks; resolve ids clinic-scoped and reload mutable rows after acquiring locks. This lets the final complete-visit comparison serialize with staff changes, including reschedules of an existing id, without changing staff permissions. No new appointment version column is required; compare the captured fields. Sender ids or body fields cannot change the contact/patient/clinic being acted on. Dispatcher fences are separate from these business locks.

## Verification scenarios

Automated tests must prove both starts, all three source kinds, existing-visit acknowledgement including staff booking during a dialogue, earliest ordering/paging and filter changes, wrong/stale/expired codes, STOP across restart, staff pause/reply/resume, inactive/unknown/shared-number limits, and no booking before CONFIRM. Cover conflict savepoints, staff moves/cancellation followed by STATUS, queued obsolete menus, confirmation delivery failure, duplicate callbacks and concurrent confirmations. Use fake-provider callback tests without staff cookies for patient actions. Staff route role/tenancy tests cover purpose and automation controls. Human proof follows the same paths with synthetic records and dedicated linked test phones.

The 2026-11-04 review adds these required scenarios:

| Gap | Required automated proof |
| --- | --- |
| Delayed control commands | Deliver BOOK after a later STOP/takeover; missing/tied timestamps keep the pause, a fresh BOOK code resumes, and expired/replayed/cross-contact codes fail. Reverse STOP/BOOK delivery and verify conservative pausing. |
| Stale queued result | Book, then staff move/cancel before dispatch; initial confirmation and STATUS/repeated codes show current details/as-of time, with exactly one visit and booking audit/timeline sequence. Freeze the body after claim. |
| Abandoned sender | Kill the owner after claim, restart, mark it unknown without resend and dispatch a later STATUS; other contacts proceed. A live fenced owner past its deadline cannot be overlapped or finalized by an old owner. |
| Changed acknowledged visit | Move an acknowledged visit without changing its id; require refreshed acknowledgement/summary/code. Race confirmation against staff reschedule/cancel/status and retain current staff role/status rules without deadlock. |
| Free text | Unknown text during booking stores history, flags needs_staff, invalidates choices and sends only fixed handoff copy; recognisable invalid codes recover, and paused/post-booking ordinary conversation does not restart booking. |
| Complete bounded pages | More than 20 visits cannot hide a new visit from final validation; more than five visits/types/clinicians, long labels and all slot pages fit 2,000 characters with stable paging, no omissions and no acknowledgement of unseen entries. |

Vitest covers resume-challenge copy/states, generic handoff, multi-page acknowledgement and staff-send replay after patient resume without taking over again. These remain build-time tests; this document does not claim they have run.
