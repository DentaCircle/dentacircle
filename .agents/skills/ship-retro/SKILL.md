# Retro

Find one useful change to the process, or say none is needed.

## Read the evidence

Use done and built briefs, ROADMAP.md, `.shipit/open.md`, git history, and the
conversation. Do not claim to have seen a session you cannot access.

Look for concrete friction:

- Review found problems the checks should have caught.
- Briefs that were too big, too vague, or approved and then changed.
- Questions that asked for information already recorded.
- Steps skipped because they cost more than they gave.
- Findings lost between sessions.
- Estimates that were far off, and why.

The developer's report of friction counts as evidence even from a single event.

## Propose one change

Prefer deleting or relaxing a rule over adding one. Give the file, the current text,
the replacement, and the evidence. Apply it only when the developer says so.
Suggest a small next slice to try it on and what to watch for.

Use plain words and short instructions.
