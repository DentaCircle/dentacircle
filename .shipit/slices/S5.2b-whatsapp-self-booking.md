# S5.2b Patient booking conversation

Status: approved
Branch: slice/S5.2b-whatsapp-self-booking
Size: M

## Goal

A linked patient starts a chat or answers a receptionist's invitation, then chooses and confirms an appointment; reception can take over when needed.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Both entry points.** Patient messages and the post-registration staff invitation lead to the same booking flow. `BOOK` starts/resumes booking, subject to paused-contact freshness checks in criterion 4; a greeting gets a welcome with BOOK/STAFF instructions. Ordinary conversation after a booking does not start another booking. Identity always comes from the verified clinic/account/contact link. Unknown/inactive senders cannot book.
2. **Existing visits first.** Show all current upcoming appointments through bounded pages before offering another; do not reuse the staff read's 20-visit cap. Creating an additional visit requires an explicit Book another visit choice after the complete list. Record the acknowledged visits' ids, starts/ends, clinician, type and status. Recheck at confirmation: new visits, changed membership or changed fields (even the same id after a staff move) invalidate the acknowledgement and confirmation code. Show refreshed details and require a new summary/confirmation, plus an additional-booking choice if visits remain. A booked follow-up/recall shows its visit rather than another booking action. General visits never silently close a queue row; only the patient's explicit scheduled/contacted source is booked.
3. **Automatic earliest choices and bounded pages.** After the patient chooses a source and active appointment type, automatically offer the earliest slots across active clinicians over the next seven clinic-local days. Exclude the patient's conflicts; sort by start, then clinician id. Page every visit/type/clinician/slot list, with at most five entries and the entire message within S5.1's 2,000-character limit, including tokens/navigation. Use fewer entries or safe display-label shortening when needed, with unambiguous codes and full selection details in the summary. MORE continues via a version-bound cursor; no hidden entries or silent truncation. Change date/clinician/type offers fresh choices within the existing horizon. No slots, no clinicians/types and Not sure what to book have a route to reception. No procedure/duration is inferred from care notes.
4. **Choice, confirmation and fresh resume.** Bind short menu tokens to sender/account/session/menu version and contact automation version. Selecting a slot sends a summary and distinct CONFIRM code; only that command books. Choices expire after 15 idle minutes. Changing choices invalidates prior codes. BOOK resumes an active session; START OVER clears active choices. STOP ends the flow and persists across expiry/restart; it never cancels a visit. Stale events cannot clear a later pause/takeover. When reliable event ordering cannot prove a paused sender's BOOK is fresh (including missing/coarse/tied timestamps), keep the pause and issue a short-lived `BOOK <code>` challenge bound to its current automation version. Only that fresh code or staff resume restores active mode and fresh choices. Callback arrival order alone is insufficient; see the booking contract's conservative fallback.
5. **Reception takeover and free text.** STAFF/Talk to reception pauses menus and visibly flags the inbox/conversation. During an active booking flow, unrecognised free text does the same with one fixed, non-clinical handoff reply; no semantic classification or treatment advice. Recognisable invalid/stale code syntax gets safe recovery instead. Staff can take over or resume; an ordinary staff reply also takes over. Booking invitations are explicitly identified and do not take over. While paused, free text stays in history without bot menus; old confirmation codes cannot book. Fresh patient resume per criterion 4 or staff resume clears the pause and starts fresh choices. No automatic hand-back on timeout. Show who is handling the chat; copy says reception can review and clinical questions need a clinician, without promising an immediate response or external notification.
6. **Final validation.** Lock patient before contact/session/appointment/source rows, consistent with S5.2a's staff mutation locks. Recheck active identity, chosen source, clinician/type, current hours/duration, overlaps, both versions and the complete acknowledged visit snapshot under that patient lock. Invalid choices create no partial appointment/source/audit/timeline writes and get recovery options. Reuse existing booking services and S5.2a actors; appointment status stays scheduled. Text/invitation alone never marks contacted.
7. **Durability and truthful recovery.** Commit inbound text, conversation transition and queued reply together. A slot conflict rolls back the attempted booking while retaining the incoming text and recovery reply. Duplicate/concurrent confirmations create one visit. Send its reference after commit. Suppress queued obsolete/expired menus or menus paused by staff; preserve the booking result's reference, refreshing any time/status details from the saved visit immediately before dispatch and recording the as-of time. Never send a cached booked claim after staff move/cancellation. STATUS and repeated confirmation use that same fresh result path without recreating the visit. S5.2a abandoned-claim recovery lets later STATUS replies proceed without retrying an uncertain send. Provider failure never rolls back or rebooks.
8. **Registration and invitation.** On successful registration, open the saved patient's detail page with Saved/Open WhatsApp. Explicitly link the test number, preview and send a clinic-identifying invitation asking for BOOK; existing patients can be invited too. Registration sends nothing by itself. Link/send failure leaves the patient saved. An uncertain registration save directs staff to check the patient list before retrying, rather than claiming it failed. Preserve send request-id behavior. Editing patient.phone never changes the WhatsApp link; shared/changed numbers use reception booking under S5.1's immutable one-number/one-patient limit.

- UI/docs: post-save route uses the id already returned by POST /patients; add invitation preview, handling badges and Take over/Resume controls to S5.1 screens. Keep manual Refresh; no unread/notification system. Reuse calendar/queue/timeline. Detailed commands, state transitions and recovery: [booking contract](../../docs/whatsapp-booking.md).

## Out of scope

Patient registration by chat, number reassignment/shared-family identity, NLP/AI/clinical advice, campaigns, automatic registration sends, reminders, patient cancellation/rescheduling, reservations, new providers/dependencies and real patient use.

### Decisions made

- Every paused bare BOOK requires a short-lived challenge. Provider timestamps cannot prove
  cross-channel ordering, so callback arrival order does not clear a pause.
- A general booking never selects a follow-up or recall automatically. A booked source is
  represented by its visit in the upcoming list.
- A queued result is refreshed under the patient lock immediately before the durable send
  claim. The claim is released before the provider call; it does not promise delivery-time
  freshness.
- Invitations use the clinic's fixed preview and are distinguished from ordinary staff
  messages in request-id replay. The latter take over only on the first request.
- The local dispatcher retains S5.2a's same-host process fence; it is not a distributed lock.
- WhatsApp menu headings, spacing, reply instructions, existing appointments and confirmation
  layout are clearer; patient-facing appointment summaries no longer show internal IDs.
  Numbered choices require a reply to the current accepted WhatsApp message. Internal menu
  tokens remain for backward compatibility but are not displayed to patients.

### Unsure points

- The long booking service and command wording deserve review for readability and usability.
- Plain numeric replies cannot distinguish a late response to an old menu. Patients must use
  WhatsApp's reply gesture; provider responses without a message ID cannot authenticate choices.

  The developer later removed the synthetic chat and the provider confirmed it was absent;

- An interrupted or rejected provider send cannot establish delivery. STATUS refreshes the
