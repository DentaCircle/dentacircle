# S5.2 WhatsApp booking

Status: approved
Branch: see child briefs
Size: two M slices

## Goal

An existing linked patient starts a chat or replies to a receptionist's booking invitation, then chooses and confirms an appointment entirely in WhatsApp.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An active, linked, allowlisted test sender enters the fixed booking menu. Show all existing visits through bounded pages before an explicit Book another visit choice; never inherit the staff read's 20-visit cap. All visit/type/clinician/slot pages respect five entries and the 2,000-character limit. The patient chooses a general visit or bookable follow-up/recall and appointment type, then sees earliest availability automatically, with optional clinician/date filters.
2. The patient selects a slot, reviews a summary and explicitly confirms. Recheck the slot and the acknowledged visits' membership, times, clinician, type and status, including staff moves/cancellation, then save one scheduled appointment, updating only the explicitly selected follow-up/recall. Changed visits require refreshed acknowledgement and confirmation. Not sure/no slots leads to reception help.
3. Duplicate callbacks, repeated confirmations and provider uncertainty never create duplicate bookings. Suppress obsolete queued menus; stale/taken choices get recovery options. Send a reference after commit, refreshing any time/status claim immediately before dispatch. STATUS/repeated codes show the current visit without rebooking. Recover abandoned sending claims as unknown, without resending them or blocking later replies indefinitely.
4. The sender can act only for the patient linked to that clinic/account/number. Unknown or inactive senders cannot book. No clinical interpretation; staff can read the conversation in the inbox.
5. After saving registration and explicitly linking the WhatsApp number, a receptionist can send a welcome/booking invitation identifying the clinic and asking the patient to reply `BOOK`. It uses S5.1's staff send flow and starts the same booking dialogue when the patient replies. Registration itself sends nothing, and link/send failure never undoes registration. Sending alone changes no appointment or queue status.
6. Talk to reception and unrecognised free text during an active booking flow pause menus and visibly flag the conversation, with fixed non-clinical handoff copy. Staff takeover/reply also pauses; invitations are a separate purpose. STOP persists across expiry/restart. Only a fresh patient BOOK or staff resume restores the booking flow, with fresh choices. Delayed/unorderable BOOK while paused requires a new version-bound BOOK code; arrival order or a missing/coarse provider timestamp never proves freshness.

## Out of scope

Patient self-registration, automatic messages on registration, shared-family-number/relinking support, real patient use, AI/free-text understanding, clinical advice, unattended campaigns, reminders (S5.3), patient booking changes/cancellation by chat, new providers/paid services and slot reservations. STOP does not cancel a visit.

## Decisions and risks

**Approved 2026-11-03, including both entry points:** the patient can start the chat, or the receptionist can send a booking invitation after registration/linking so the patient receives the clinic's number. The patient chooses and confirms; no receptionist booking approval. First version serves registered patients with explicitly linked test numbers. The split and child briefs are approved with this refinement; decisions are recorded in PRODUCT.md.

**Chat protocol:** fixed text menus/codes, up to five choices per message and 15-minute selections. Review choices accepted 2026-11-03: show existing visits before explicit additional booking; offer earliest slots over seven days before optional filters; pause/flag reception handoff with explicit resume. Tradeoff: structured replies; no AI/provider-specific buttons, extra dependency or deployment service. [Workflow review](../../docs/reviews/S5.2-workflow-review.md) records gaps and recovery requirements; [booking contract](../../docs/whatsapp-booking.md) gives details.

**Review corrections authorised 2026-11-04:** stale control-command protection, dispatch-time result freshness, abandoned-claim recovery, acknowledgement of changed visits, deterministic free-text handoff and complete pagination are included in the child briefs. The child briefs remain approved.

**Prerequisite:** finish and verify S5.1, which is approved but unbuilt here. Audit/timeline currently require a staff user, so patient booking needs actor/schema changes. Do not fabricate a staff actor. Nothing in `.shipit/open.md` blocks planning; preserve unrelated checkout changes.

## How it will be verified

Child briefs map criteria to PostgreSQL service/API tests and Vitest. Prove both starts, existing-visit acknowledgement, earliest choices and reception takeover/resume. Check STOP, obsolete queued menus and current STATUS after a staff move/cancel. Inspect calendar/queue/timeline; repeat conflicts, confirmation and disconnection. CI uses a fake provider; report unavailable live checks.

Also prove reversed/delayed BOOK and STOP/takeover, missing/tied provider timestamps, expired/replayed resume codes, staff mutation before queued-result dispatch, killed sender followed by STATUS, a changed visit with the same id, free-text handoff, and complete pages with more than five choices, long labels and more than 20 visits.

Visual proof is listed per child in `.shipit/proof/S5.2a/` and `.shipit/proof/S5.2b/`, with test-account identifiers hidden.
